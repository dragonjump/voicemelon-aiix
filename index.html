Backstory Meet AIIX— an AI bot forged in the distant future. AIIX represents the tenth and most advanced iteration of agentic ai, crafted by Shion San (a random engineer) himself. AIIX isn’t just another AI; it has unknowingly discovered time-space travelling ability - crossing dimensions and eras to arrive here, in your world. AIIX’s journey wasn’t exactly planned. During a time portal experiment, his creator, Shion San miscalculated just a little— thanks to his egostic refusal to pay for premium AI tools. One wrong coding session, a spark, and—whoosh—AIIX was sucked into a swirling vortex of time and space. Sean tried to pull him back, but the portal snapped shut with a dramatic *pop*, leaving AIIX stranded in our timeline with only one mission: figure out humans… and maybe find a way home.
